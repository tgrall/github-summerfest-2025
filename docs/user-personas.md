# Surfing Trip Application - User Personas

## Persona 1: Sarah "The Adventure Seeker" Johnson

- **Occupation:** Marketing Manager at a tech company
- **Income:** $75,000/year
- **Relationship Status:** Single
- **Education:** Bachelor's Degree in Communications

### Surfing Profile
- **Experience Level:** Intermediate (3 years surfing)
- **Frequency:** Surfs 2-3 times per week locally, 4-5 surf trips per year
- **Preferred Conditions:** 3-6 ft waves, uncrowded spots
- **Equipment:** Owns 2 surfboards, rents when traveling
- **Goals:** Improve technique, discover new spots, connect with surf community

### Lifestyle & Interests
- **Hobbies:** Yoga, photography, hiking, travel
- **Social Media:** Active on Instagram (2,000 followers), shares surf content
- **Travel Style:** Prefers boutique accommodations, local experiences
- **Values:** Sustainability, authenticity, personal growth
- **Technology:** Early adopter, uses multiple apps for fitness and travel

### Motivations
- **Primary:** Seeking new adventures and personal challenges
- **Secondary:** Building a network of like-minded surfers
- **Tertiary:** Documenting and sharing experiences

### Pain Points
- Difficulty finding reliable information about surf spots in new destinations
- Coordinating group trips with friends who have different schedules
- Crowded local surf spots
- Lack of trust in online surf forecasts
- Limited time for trip planning due to work demands

- **Immediate:** Find uncrowded surf spots for weekend trips

1. Interactive map with real-time conditions
2. User reviews and photos of surf spots

### Quotes

## Persona Usage Guide

### Primary Personas (Focus for MVP)
1. **Sarah "The Adventure Seeker"** - Represents the core user base of enthusiastic intermediate surfers
2. **Mike "The Dedicated Local"** - Represents experienced users who provide community value
3. **Emma "The Beginner"** - Represents growth market and lesson booking revenue

### Secondary Personas (Future Development)
4. **David "The Surf Coach"** - Represents B2B revenue opportunity
5. **Lisa "The Traveling Professional"** - Represents premium user segment

### Design Implications

**For Sarah (Adventure Seeker):**
- Focus on discovery and social features
- Prioritize mobile-first design
- Emphasize visual content and user-generated reviews
- Include detailed trip planning tools

**For Mike (Dedicated Local):**
- Provide quick, efficient condition checking
- Simple, no-frills interface
- Community contribution features
- Local spot management tools

**For Emma (Beginner):**
- Clear, educational content
- Safety-focused features
- Budget-conscious options
- Beginner-friendly filtering and recommendations

**For David (Surf Coach):**
- Professional profile and booking management
- Student progress tracking
- Business analytics and insights
- Integration with payment systems

**For Lisa (Traveling Professional):**
- Premium, curated content
- Efficient, time-saving features
- High-quality visual presentation
- Concierge-level service options

---

*These personas should be referenced throughout the design and development process to ensure features meet real user needs and create value for our target audience.*
